## Retrospective (what to improve)
- **Weakest dimensions: Pacing (4) and Prose (4)** — the same two dimensions that scored 4 in run 01, both improved in kind but not to 5.
  - Pacing: ch04 and ch05 are still ~2,100 consecutive words whose present-time action is standing up and crossing to a window; and chunks 03–07 all close on the same choreography (a hand at rest on a surface plus a withheld understanding). Content repetition (the run-01 doorway restage and answering-night retell) is gone; *cadence* repetition replaced it.
  - Prose: the "the way …" tic was cut from ~38 to 10 (inside the §5c cap), but the underlying analogy construction was largely *renamed* rather than removed — the family (`the way a / much as / as a <noun> <verb> / as though / as if / like a`) still runs ~1 per 400 words, and a new member "was itself a kind of" appeared ×3.
- **Recurring pattern vs prior run:** Pacing and Prose were the run-01 4s too (Pacing was the sole sub-4 on the Grok/Gemini/GPT panel reads). The deterministic §5c gates successfully killed the *surface* forms the run-01 eval named (dash/spelling/"the way" string/"small competent"/doorway restage), which is why Arc and Voice rose to 5 — but the *structural* habits (analogy-routing of interior states; uniform chunk-closing cadence; adjacent-seam echo) survive because no deterministic check targets them yet.
- **Learnings promoted to spec this run:** v4 itself was the promotion of run-01's findings (the §4 reveal ledger, the §5c whole-work gates, the explicit §7 relief-keeper fact, the extended §5a UK grep). They landed: reveal-ownership is clean per chunk, spelling drift is resolved, and both `toward` hard-fails are gone. No new learnings were appended to `learnings.md` this run except the reviewer's ch03 entry (ledger chunks must not extend their verdict onto the current machine's fidelity — that is ch06's beat).
- **Suggested spec/steering changes (concrete, from the eval's whole-work observations):**
  1. **Widen the §5c signature-construction cap** from the literal string `the way …` to the whole analogy *family* (`the way|much as|as a <noun> <verb>|as though|as if|like a`), so the cap cannot be satisfied by substitution. This is the single highest-value change; it directly targets the surviving Prose defect.
  2. **Add a §5c chunk-closing-cadence check:** flag when N consecutive chunks end on the same gesture/construction (e.g. a repeated "hand at rest + and understood/did not know"). Targets the surviving Pacing defect.
  3. **Add a §5c seam-adjacency near-duplicate check:** compare each chunk's closing paragraph against the next chunk's opening paragraph (the ch08→ch09 handoff repeated an opening clause and re-narrated the last mark; whole-work substring detection missed it because it concentrates at one seam).

The §7 coverage fact itself is now correct and explicit, but the book-count rate is loose; a one-clause change in ch03 would close it.
