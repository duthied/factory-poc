### Judgment on the two declared intentional echoes

Both were assessed on the merits and both are judged to earn their keep.

- **"no sense in hurrying the last..."** (ch01 "she had come a long way to look and there seemed no sense in hurrying the last part" / ch02 "she had come this far in a single afternoon and there seemed no sense in hurrying the last of it"). Works. Two uses is deliberate patterning, not a tic, and the second use does something the first cannot: it applies her unhurriedness to the drawer she is avoiding, so the same phrase reads as composure on the hill and as evasion at the table. Keep.
- **"turning its attention towards a shore..."** (ch05 "would go on turning its attention towards a shore that had quietly stopped speaking to it" / ch06 "had gone on turning its attention towards a shore that no longer knew how to answer it"). Works, and is the single most load-bearing echo in the piece: ch05 is the imagined cost inside a conditional she cannot test, ch06 is the same sentence with the conditional removed. The variation in the tail clause ("stopped speaking" to "no longer knew how to answer") is what makes it a return rather than a repeat. Keep.

A third undeclared 2x echo was found and is also judged sound: "the sea doing what it always did, working at the rock, indifferent, enormous" (ch04) recurs verbatim in ch07 ("looked down at the sea doing what it always did at that hour, working at the rock below, indifferent, enormous") specifically so that the next sentence's deviation registers against an established baseline: "Except that... the working seemed to slacken." ### Non-blocking observations (for the retrospective and `learnings.md`, not for revision)

The verdict is PASS; nothing below is a revision instruction. Recorded because each
is a whole-work defect a per-chunk reviewer structurally cannot catch.

- **Logbook arithmetic wrinkle (ch02 vs ch03).** ch02 establishes one book per year of her keeping ("one for every year of her keeping"). ch03 then has her reach past her final book for "the two that followed it" and describes them as kept by "Other hands... decade upon decade". Two books cannot hold the ~17 relief-keeper years at one book per year, and "decade upon decade" slightly overstates 17 years. The §7 relief-keeper coverage itself is now correct and explicit in both ch03 and ch06 ("One relief keeper after another had held it for the whole of the seventeen years after that"), so the canon fix landed; only the book-count rate is loose. A one-clause change in ch03 would close it.
- **The signature-construction cap needs widening (§5c).** The check greps only the literal string "the way ...", and the writer duly cut that from ~38 to 10 while the same rhetorical move persisted at ~1 per 400 words under other openers, and a fresh variant ("was itself a kind of" x3) appeared in the gap. A cap on a single surface string will keep being satisfied by substitution. Future runs should cap the *construction family* (`the way|much as|as a <noun> <verb>|as though|as if|like a`) rather than one phrasing of it.
- **Chunk-closing cadence is unchecked.** Five consecutive chunks (03 to 07) end on a hand at rest plus a withheld understanding. Each is individually good and individually invisible; assembled, it is the clearest remaining pacing defect. Worth a deterministic §5c check on the last sentence of each chunk (repeated closing gesture or repeated "and understood/did not know" construction).
- **Seam-adjacency check missing.** ch08's last paragraph and ch09's first paragraph both open "The sky beyond the lamp room glass had..." and both narrate the last mark of the watch. §5c's near-duplicate detection compares long shared substrings across the whole; it should also specifically compare each chunk's closing paragraph against the next chunk's opening paragraph, where handoff repetition concentrates.
- **Verbatim triple of the recolour image.** "flung wide beneath a sky the colour of wet slate" appears three times (ch01, ch09, ch10), which trips the letter of the §5c distinctive-phrase rule (3+ words, 3+ occurrences) while serving the §4 requirement that ch09 call back and ch10 recolour the ch01 horizon. It is judged sound here, because ch09 marks it as identical on purpose ("the same grey plate... unchanged, indifferent") and ch10 explicitly sets it aside ("was not the horizon from the hill"). Noted only because the §5c check and the §4 callback requirement are in direct tension on this phrase, and the tension should be resolved in the spec rather than adjudicated ad hoc each run.
