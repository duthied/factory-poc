- [2026-08-30] (from chunk-02) Before stating any numeric age or duration for Maren, check it against §7 canon arithmetic: arrival at 19, departure at 41, so any span describing "years of keeping" has a hard ceiling of 22 years (likely less, since becoming keeper followed arrival by some interval). Prefer a vaguer phrase ("more than twenty years") over a precise number that canon math could contradict.
- [2026-08-30] (from chunk-03) A ledger/log-discovery chunk may conclude with a verdict about the *log's* or the *light's historical* purpose (not for the ships), but must not extend that verdict to the *present, automated* light's fidelity or failure ("keeps the letter but is deaf to the substance" is the ch06-owned beat per the §4 reveal ledger). Hedging the claim as the character's own undefendable certainty does not exempt it: the hedge softens whether she is right, not whether the proposition itself was stated. Check every ledger-chunk's closing lines against whether they paraphrase a later chunk's owned beat, especially the automation's-failure beat, which is easy to reach for as a poetic capstone.
